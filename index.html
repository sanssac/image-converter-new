Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online image converter completely free?
Yes, Image Lab Converter is 100% free with no file size limits, no daily caps, no registration, and no watermarks.
Are my images secure and private?
Absolutely. Our converter runs 100% client-side inside your browser via HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never uploaded, transmitted, or saved to any external server, ensuring total privacy for sensitive documents and personal photos.
What image formats are supported?
We support a comprehensive array of modern, popular, and vector image formats including JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF, SVG, HEIC (iPhone photos), TIFF, ICO (favicons), and PDF document compilation.
Does converting images reduce quality?
No. When converting between lossless formats like PNG, WebP, or SVG, quality is fully preserved. When converting to JPEG or compressing images, you have precise control over quality and target file size.
How do I convert HEIC photos from iPhone without quality loss?
Simply drag and drop your Apple .heic files directly into Image Lab Converter. Our built-in decoder decodes the full-resolution photo client-side and outputs high-quality JPG or PNG files instantly on Windows, Mac, or Android.
Can I convert multiple files in batch and download them as a ZIP?
Yes. You can select or drop multiple images at once. Once converted, you can download them individually or compile all processed images into a single ZIP archive or PDF document with one click.
